What makes it different

Designed around the demands of your week.

FAME's concierge service is not a tier of premium extras. It is the foundational service model for business clients who require dentistry to fit around their life — not the other way around.

This means PA-to-PA scheduling, direct clinical access, proactive annual reviews, and a single named clinician who knows your history. Most of our business clients have never had this from a dental practice before. Most would not go back to the conventional model.

Four things we do differently.

Not marketing positions. Operational commitments that are written into how the practice works.

PA-led enquiry

Every enquiry is read by a real person. Our PA calls back within two hours. Where appropriate, the initial conversation is PA-to-PA — no hold music, no booking portal.

Your calendar, not ours

Appointments are scheduled around your diary. Early morning and late evening available. We accommodate board meeting schedules, travel, and deadline periods.

One named clinician

Never an associate. You will see the same person at every appointment. They will know your history, your anxieties, and your preferences without needing to be reminded.

Direct clinical access

WhatsApp access to your clinician for urgent clinical questions. Annual proactive health review — you won't need to remember to book it.
