About FAME Dentistry

A practice built on two decades of specialist care.

Founded by Dr Ferhan Ahmed, FAME Dentistry is the practice twenty years in the making, defined by dual qualification, clinical precision, and a philosophy that places the whole patient above the procedure.

The Practice

Decidedly different from the first consultation.

FAME Dentistry is a specialist implant and cosmetic dental practice with a concierge service for the business community, now accepting new patients on Scotland Street, Glasgow.

Every aspect of FAME has been designed to answer the failings of conventional dentistry: the anonymous associate model, the generalist claiming specialist skills, the practice that treats time as a billing unit rather than a resource.

FAME does none of those things. What it does, it does once, and does properly.

The Clinicians

The people behind every procedure.

Dual-qualified specialists leading from the front, setting a new standard for complex dental care. Founder

Dr Ferhan Ahmed

BDS  ·  MFDS  ·  MBChB

"I went back to study medicine because I wanted to understand the whole patient, not just the teeth. Twenty years later, that philosophy still shapes every procedure I do."

One of a small number of UK dentists with both medical and dental qualifications. International speaker and mentor to implant dentists across the UK and Europe. A practice twenty years in the making.

FAME Dentistry opens on Scotland Street, Glasgow in Summer 2026. The location is deliberate: close to the business community it serves, designed from the ground up to operate as a concierge practice, not retrofitted.

"I've spent twenty years thinking about what dentistry could feel like if it were designed around the patient rather than around the practitioner's day. FAME is the practice I've been working towards throughout my career."

Dr Ferhan Ahmed

Until FAME opens, Dr Ferhan Ahmed continues to practise in Edinburgh, where patients requiring immediate care are welcome.
