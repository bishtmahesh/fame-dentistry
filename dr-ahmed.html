The Practice

Decidedly different from the first consultation.

FAME Dentistry is a specialist implant and cosmetic dental practice with a concierge service for the business community — now accepting new patients on Scotland Street, Glasgow.

Every aspect of FAME has been designed to answer the failings of conventional private dentistry: the anonymous associate model, the generalist claiming specialist skills, the practice that treats time as a billing unit rather than a resource.

FAME does none of those things. What it does, it does once, and does properly.

Founder

Dr Ferhan Ahmed

BDS  ·  MFDS  ·  MBChB

Implants & pre-surgical optimisation · International lecturer and mentor

One of a small number of UK dentists with both medical and dental qualifications. International speaker and mentor to implant dentists across the UK and Europe. Dr Ferhan Ahmed's dual qualification enables pre-surgical medical optimisation as a standard part of complex implant cases — something most dental practices do not offer.

He is passionate about pushing the boundaries of dental implantology, sharing his expertise globally while continuing to treat complex cases that other clinics turn away. His vision for FAME Dentistry is to build a practice that delivers unparalleled clinical excellence and patient care.
