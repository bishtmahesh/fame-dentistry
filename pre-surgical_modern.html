Pre-Surgical Optimisation · Glasgow

The workup most dentists can't carry out.

Dr Ferhan Ahmed's dual medical and dental qualification enables pre-surgical assessment that reduces risk and improves long-term implant outcomes. It's not available at most practices. It's standard at FAME.

Why it matters

Surgery is the last step. Preparation is the work.

Most implant failures are not surgical errors. They are the result of conditions that weren't identified before the procedure began.

Uncontrolled blood sugar, compromised bone density, undiagnosed clotting disorders, these are discoverable. But only if the clinician has the medical training to look for them. Dr Ferhan Ahmed's MBChB qualification means he brings physician-level assessment into the dental chair.

The result: fewer complications, better healing, and implants that last. Not as an aspiration, as a clinical standard.

The Assessment

What pre-surgical optimisation includes.

Each assessment is tailored to your clinical profile. These are the components most commonly carried out.

01.

Blood Panel

HbA1c, full blood count, coagulation markers. Ordered and interpreted by Dr Ferhan Ahmed in his capacity as a physician. Identifies conditions that must be managed before surgery proceeds.

02.

Bone Density Review

CBCT scan analysed for bone volume, density, and quality at the proposed implant site. Determines whether grafting is required, and what type.

03.

Medical History Assessment

Systematic review of medications, chronic conditions, and surgical history. Many common medications affect healing and osseointegration, these interactions are identified upfront.

04.

Risk Stratification

Patient risk profile documented against surgical complexity. Determines timing, sedation requirements, and whether specialist medical liaison is needed before proceeding.

05.

Medication Review

Assessment of bisphosphonates, anticoagulants, immunosuppressants, and other medications with known dental surgical interactions. Managed proactively, not discovered intraoperatively.

06.

Patient Optimisation Plan

Where findings indicate, blood sugar management, smoking cessation support, nutritional protocol. Surgery proceeds only when the patient is in the best possible condition for it.

The patients who benefit most.

Pre-surgical optimisation is relevant for any implant patient. But it's especially important for those whose cases carry elevated risk, and who may have been told elsewhere that implants aren't possible.

·

Type 2 diabetics, HbA1c management before and after placement significantly improves osseointegration rates.

·

Patients on anticoagulants, managed safely with the right protocol; not a contraindication when properly assessed.

·

Previous implant failures, often traceable to an underlying condition that wasn't investigated first.

·

Complex full-arch cases, where the surgical margin for error is zero and preparation is everything.

·

Patients with significant medical histories, who have avoided treatment because no one has given them a confident clinical plan.

Questions

What patients ask about this service.

Does every implant patient need pre-surgical optimisation?

Every FAME implant patient receives a pre-surgical medical assessment, the depth of workup depends on their individual medical profile. For straightforward, healthy patients, the assessment is lighter. For patients with relevant medical history, it is comprehensive.

I've been told I'm not suitable for implants. Should I get a second opinion?

Yes. Many patients told they're unsuitable have underlying conditions that, when properly managed, make implant placement not only possible but predictable. The first consultation will give you a clear, honest answer, including alternatives if implants genuinely aren't the right route.

Does this add significantly to the treatment timeline?

The workup typically adds one to three weeks before surgery proceeds. In almost all cases, this is the right trade. Surgery on an unprepared patient takes longer to heal, carries higher risk of failure, and often results in costly revision.

Is this available to patients referred from other practices?

Yes. Many referrals to FAME are specifically for pre-surgical assessment, independent of whether implant surgery will be carried out here. We provide a full written report to the referring clinician.

Who This Is For

  • Full medication list including supplements and OTC medicines
  • Summary of chronic conditions or recent diagnoses
  • Any previous CBCT scans or dental X-rays
  • Details of previous implant failures, if relevant
  • GP letters or specialist correspondence if available
