Your patient remains your patient.

We accept referrals from general dentists across Scotland. We do not practise routine dentistry. Every patient we see is returned to you — with a discharge note, a clinical summary, and without exception.

How referrals work.

Clear, consistent, and documented at every stage. You'll know where your patient is throughout.

Step 01

You refer

Submit the referral form below or call Dr Ferhan Ahmed directly. Include any relevant imaging or clinical notes.

Step 02

Acknowledgement

We acknowledge your referral within one working day and confirm the appointment date.

Step 03

Patient seen

Dr Ferhan Ahmed or Dr Tariq Bashir sees your patient. Clinician-to-clinician call available before or after.

Step 04

Discharge

Discharge note to you within 5 working days. Your patient is returned to your care for all routine treatment.
