Methodology

How the score is collected.

Every patient who completes treatment at FAME is sent a short satisfaction survey by Customer Thermometer, an independent third-party feedback platform, not administered or scored by the practice itself.

Patients are asked a single core question: how likely they are to recommend FAME to a colleague, friend, or family member, scored 0–10. The published figure is the average of all responses received in the current measurement period, calculated and hosted by Customer Thermometer, then republished here unedited.

No response is excluded, edited, or selectively published. Milestones (Highest in Scotland → UK → Europe) are dated and sourced, and available on request for verification.
