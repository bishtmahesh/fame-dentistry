First Impressions, Final Decisions
A book by Dr Ferhan Ahmed · For Business Leaders
Mailed, Not Downloaded

In business, the first decision about you is made in a tenth of a second.

First impressions form in around a hundred milliseconds — before you speak, before your credentials are on the table. What's being judged in that instant is how much certainty you project. Certainty has been shown to leak through four channels: your body language, how you sound, how you dress, and your smile. The first three, most leaders learn to manage. The smile is the one they tend to work around rather than address. Drawing on research you won't have seen, Dr Ferhan Ahmed shows how each of the four channels shapes the impression you make — and why the fourth, the smile, is the one most leaders never address. If you are a business leader, we'd be glad to mail you a physical copy of this book.
