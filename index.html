200+ Referring Dentists

“Most of our implant patients and the advanced dental work we undertake are referred to us by other dentists.”

I've spent twenty years thinking about what dentistry could feel like if it were designed around the patient rather than around the practitioner's day. FAME is the practice I've been working towards throughout my career.

After qualifying as a dentist, I went back to study medicine because I wanted to understand more than just teeth — I wanted to understand the whole patient. That broader perspective continues to shape every treatment plan, procedure and conversation we have at FAME.
