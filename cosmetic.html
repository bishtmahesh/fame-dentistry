Our Approach

Never cosmetic for the sake of cosmetic.

At FAME, we don't offer a menu of treatments and ask which you'd like. We assess what's clinically sound, and then explore what's aesthetically desirable, in that order.

This matters because the most common regret in cosmetic dentistry isn't the treatment chosen, it's the treatment that wasn't assessed properly first. A veneer over an untreated bite issue will fail. A whitening course that ignores erosion will cause harm.

FAME's cosmetic work is led by Dr Tariq Bashir, whose background in restorative dentistry means every aesthetic decision is grounded in structural integrity.

Questions

What patients ask before starting treatment.

How do I know if I need cosmetic treatment, or something else first?

A consultation with Dr Tariq Bashir starts with a full bite and structural assessment, not a cosmetic wish list. If there's an underlying issue, that's addressed first, before any cosmetic work is discussed.

What's the difference between bonding and veneers?

Composite bonding is reversible, more affordable, and can often be completed in a single visit, but needs more maintenance over time. Porcelain veneers last longer and resist staining better, but involve some tooth preparation. Which is right for you is discussed at consultation.

My previous composite bonding failed. Can you fix it?

Often, yes. We assess why it failed first, commonly a bite issue or grinding, before proposing a replacement, so the same problem doesn't happen again.

How long do cosmetic results last?

Composite bonding typically lasts 5 to 7 years with good care, and veneers 10 to 15 years or longer. Longevity depends heavily on whether any underlying bite or grinding issues were addressed first.

Is cosmetic treatment painful?

Most cosmetic procedures, including bonding and veneer preparation, are minimally invasive and carried out under local anaesthetic where needed. Whitening causes no discomfort for most patients.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, finance options are available and discussed at your consultation, alongside full fee transparency before any treatment begins.

What if I'm not happy with the planned result?

We use a digital mock-up or trial smile before committing to permanent treatment, so adjustments can be made before we proceed, not after.
