Why Choose FAME Dentistry for Pre-Surgical Optimisation

Better preparation leads to better outcomes.

Dental implants rely on far more than surgical precision alone. Healthy gums, adequate bone volume, a balanced bite, and a stable oral environment all contribute to successful healing and long-term implant performance.

At FAME Dentistry, implant treatment begins with preparation rather than procedure. Every patient undergoes a comprehensive pre-surgical assessment to identify biological, functional, and restorative factors that may influence treatment outcomes. This allows us to address existing concerns before implant placement, reducing unnecessary risks and improving treatment predictability.

Using advanced digital diagnostics, detailed clinical examination, and restorative-led planning, we assess every aspect of your oral health. Where necessary, gum disease is stabilised, damaged teeth are managed, bone quality is evaluated, and additional preparatory treatment is recommended before surgery proceeds. This methodical, evidence-based approach creates the strongest possible foundation for implant treatment, helping support successful healing, long-term stability, and restorations that continue to function comfortably for years to come.

The Process

Preparing every detail before treatment begins.

Our structured workflow ensures every stage of implant preparation is completed before surgery, creating greater confidence and more predictable outcomes.

01.

Consultation & Clinical Assessment

We begin by understanding your concerns, medical history, previous dental treatment, and long-term goals. A comprehensive examination allows us to assess your oral health and determine the most appropriate treatment pathway.

02.

Digital Diagnostics & Treatment Planning

Advanced imaging, intraoral scans, photographs, and bite analysis provide a complete understanding of your oral condition. Every clinical finding contributes to a personalised treatment plan designed around your individual needs.

03.

Biological Optimisation

Where necessary, preparatory treatment is completed before implant placement. This may include periodontal therapy, tooth extraction, ridge preservation, or other procedures designed to create healthier conditions for healing.

04.

Implant Readiness

Once your oral health has been optimised, your personalised implant treatment plan is finalised. Every stage of surgery and restoration is carefully coordinated to support precise implant placement and predictable long-term success.

Pre-Surgical Pathways

Personalised preparation for predictable implant treatment.

Every implant patient presents with different clinical needs. Following your assessment, we recommend only the treatment necessary to establish a healthy biological environment before implant placement.

01.

Comprehensive Oral Health Assessment

Every implant journey begins with a detailed evaluation of your teeth, gums, jawbone, bite, existing restorations, and overall oral health. Digital scans, photographs, and diagnostic imaging allow us to identify factors that may influence treatment planning before surgery begins.

Best for: every patient considering dental implants or full-mouth rehabilitation.

02.

Periodontal Therapy

Healthy gums provide the biological support every implant requires. Where gum disease or inflammation is present, periodontal therapy is carried out to restore healthier supporting tissues before implant placement.

Best for: patients with bleeding gums, periodontal disease, or chronic inflammation.

03.

Bone Assessment & Site Preparation

Jawbone quality and volume are carefully assessed using advanced three-dimensional imaging. Where additional support is required, procedures such as ridge preservation or bone augmentation may be recommended to improve implant stability and long-term outcomes.

Best for: patients with reduced bone volume or missing teeth that have been absent for an extended period.

04.

Tooth Extraction & Ridge Preservation

Where damaged or non-restorable teeth require removal, extraction is performed using minimally invasive techniques whenever appropriate. Preserving the surrounding bone at the time of extraction can help maintain the implant site for future treatment.

Best for: patients requiring tooth extraction before implant placement.

05.

Digital Implant Planning

Using CBCT imaging and high-resolution digital scanning, every implant is virtually planned before surgery. This restorative-led workflow allows precise positioning while considering function, aesthetics, neighbouring teeth, and long-term maintenance.

Best for: single implants, multiple implants, and full-mouth rehabilitation.

The patients who benefit most.

Pre-surgical optimisation is relevant for any implant patient. But it's especially important for those whose cases carry elevated risk, and who may have been told elsewhere that implants aren't possible.

Type 2 diabetics, HbA1c management before and after placement significantly improves osseointegration rates.

Patients on anticoagulants, managed safely with the right protocol; not a contraindication when properly assessed.

Previous implant failures, often traceable to an underlying condition that wasn't investigated first.

Complex full-arch cases, where the surgical margin for error is zero and preparation is everything.

Patients with significant medical histories, who have avoided treatment because no one has given them a confident clinical plan.

Questions

What patients ask before beginning implant treatment.

Why is pre-surgical optimisation important before dental implants?

Successful implant treatment depends on more than the surgical procedure itself. Healthy gums, sufficient bone volume, a stable bite, and careful treatment planning all contribute to predictable healing and long-term implant stability. Pre-surgical optimisation allows us to identify and manage any factors that could affect treatment before implant placement begins.

Will everyone need additional treatment before receiving dental implants?

No. Some patients are suitable for implant placement following their assessment, while others may benefit from preparatory care such as periodontal therapy, tooth extraction, or bone preservation procedures. Your treatment pathway is determined entirely by your individual clinical needs.

What happens during the initial implant assessment?

Your consultation includes a comprehensive clinical examination, assessment of your medical and dental history, digital imaging where appropriate, evaluation of your gums, jawbone, and bite, followed by a detailed discussion of your treatment options. This allows us to develop a personalised treatment plan before any surgical procedures are scheduled.

How do you determine whether I have enough bone for implants?

We assess bone quality and volume using advanced three-dimensional imaging alongside a detailed clinical examination. If sufficient healthy bone is present, implant treatment may proceed as planned. Where additional support is required, we will discuss suitable treatment options before surgery.

Why is gum health so important before implant surgery?

Healthy gum tissue provides essential support for dental implants. Active gum disease or inflammation can affect healing and increase the risk of future complications. Stabilising gum health before surgery helps create a healthier environment for long-term implant success.

How long does the pre-surgical optimisation stage usually take?

The preparation phase varies depending on your individual clinical requirements. Some patients complete assessment and proceed to implant placement within a relatively short period, while others may require staged treatment over several weeks or months to optimise their oral health before surgery.

Will I receive a complete treatment plan before deciding to proceed?

Yes. Once your assessment is complete, Dr Ferhan Ahmed will provide a personalised treatment plan outlining any recommended preparatory treatment, implant options, expected treatment stages, estimated timelines, and associated fees. This allows you to make an informed decision with confidence.
