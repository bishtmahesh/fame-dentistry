Why FAME Dentistry for Implants

Glasgow's implant specialist, from diagnostic scan to final integration.

Many implant cases move between a surgeon, a restorative dentist, and a lab technician who never meet in person. At FAME Dentistry, your case is planned and reviewed by the same clinical team throughout, from the initial scan to the final restoration. This continuity matters because implant success depends on precise coordination between the surgical placement and the crown that sits above it.

Before any treatment is proposed, we complete a full diagnostic workup: 3D imaging, bone density assessment, and bite analysis. This tells us exactly where an implant can be placed safely and how it will function once restored, rather than relying on a generic template.

We also see patients who have been told elsewhere that implants aren't an option, due to bone loss, previous extractions, or failed treatment history. These cases often still have workable solutions once properly assessed.

Implant Pathways & Protocols

Tailored implant protocols for your restorative goals.

Single Tooth Implants

A single titanium post and crown replace one missing tooth without altering the adjacent teeth. This avoids the enamel reduction required for a traditional bridge.

Best for: A single missing tooth with adequate surrounding bone.

Multiple Implant-Supported Bridges

Two or more implants support a fixed bridge spanning several missing teeth, reducing the total number of implants needed while restoring full chewing function.

Best for: Several missing teeth in the same area of the arch.

Implant-Retained Dentures

A small number of implants anchor a full denture in place, eliminating the slipping and adhesive dependency of conventional dentures.

Best for: Patients missing most or all teeth in an arch who want a more stable alternative to removable dentures.

Bone Grafting and Site Preparation

Where bone volume is insufficient, grafting material is placed to rebuild a stable foundation before implant placement proceeds.

Best for: Patients with bone loss from long-term missing teeth or previous extractions.

Implant Pathways & Protocols

What to expect at every stage, from Glasgow's implant specialists.

01.

Diagnostic Assessment

3D scans and a clinical examination determine bone quality, nerve position, and the best implant location. No surgical work happens during this visit.

02.

Treatment Planning

A precise surgical plan is mapped digitally, including implant angle, depth, and restoration design, before you commit to treatment.

03.

Implant Placement

The implant post is placed under local anaesthetic. A healing period of several months follows, allowing the implant to fuse with the jawbone.

04.

Final Restoration

Once integration is confirmed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is fitted and checked for fit, bite, and appearance.

Questions

What patients ask before committing.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular check-ups, implants can last for decades. The crown on top may need replacement over time due to normal wear, but the implant post itself is designed for long-term use.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Placement is carried out under local anaesthetic, and most patients report the recovery is more comfortable than expected. Any discomfort afterwards is typically manageable with standard pain relief.

Am I too old, or is my bone loss too severe, for implants?

Age alone rarely rules out implants. Bone loss can often be addressed with grafting. A full diagnostic assessment is the only way to know what's possible in your specific case.

How long does the entire process take?

Timelines vary depending on healing and whether grafting is needed, but the process typically spans several months from placement to final restoration.

What happens if I have gum disease?

Gum health is assessed and treated before implant placement, since stable gum tissue is necessary for long-term implant success.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Pricing is discussed transparently during your consultation, along with any available finance options.

How do I care for implants after treatment?

Implants are maintained with regular brushing, flossing, and scheduled check-ups, similar to natural teeth, to monitor the surrounding gum and bone over time.
